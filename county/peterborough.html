Peterborough: jobs tied to US demand

Peterborough is not ranked. The check of the method against the exports Statistics Canada publishes failed in the metropolitan area this county belongs to, so no figure is shown for Peterborough (method).

  • Peterborough CMA: the exports the method allocates to it are 2.70 times those Statistics Canada publishes. The check allows half to twice the published figure.

Peterborough's peer group is mixed.

For a ranked county, Ontario's jobs tied to US demand in each industry (Statistics Canada 12-10-0100) are shared among counties by where its workers live, counted by the 2021 Census (98-10-0592). Every county figure is an estimate. How the figures are made, what they assume and how the method is checked are on the methods page.
