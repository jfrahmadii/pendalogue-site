Huron: jobs tied to US demand

About 5,200 jobs held by Huron residents, 18% of all employed residents, exist because of demand from the United States (Ontario: 16%). Huron ranks 11 of 44 Ontario counties on this share. About 19% of what its industries sell to the United States paid US duty in June to August 2026 (Ontario: 25%).

Method and limits

How the figure is made

Ontario's jobs tied to US demand in each industry (Statistics Canada 12-10-0100) are shared among counties by where its workers live, counted by the 2021 Census (98-10-0592):

county's jobs tied to US demand = Ontario's jobs tied to US demand in the industry × (county's residents working in the industry ÷ Ontario's residents working in it), summed over the county's industries

The share is that total as a percent of Huron's employed residents. Each duty share averages the industries' Ontario-wide shares, each weighted by Huron's estimated US exports in the industry.

How sure

These are estimates. Huron lies outside every metropolitan area, so no published figure checks it on its own. The method is checked on the metropolitan areas and on Ontario's rural and small-town areas taken together. There it places 10.0 percent of Ontario's exports, where Statistics Canada's figures show 4.9 percent: the method counts workers where they live, Statistics Canada counts exporters where they are registered. Read this county's figures with that in mind (method).
