Waterloo: jobs tied to US demand

About 55,700 jobs held by Waterloo residents, 20% of all employed residents, exist because of demand from the United States (Ontario: 16%). Waterloo ranks 5 of 44 Ontario counties on this share. About 38% of what its industries sell to the United States paid US duty in June to August 2026 (Ontario: 25%).

Method and limits

How the figure is made

Ontario's jobs tied to US demand in each industry (Statistics Canada 12-10-0100) are shared among counties by where its workers live, counted by the 2021 Census (98-10-0592):

county's jobs tied to US demand = Ontario's jobs tied to US demand in the industry × (county's residents working in the industry ÷ Ontario's residents working in it), summed over the county's industries

The share is that total as a percent of Waterloo's employed residents. Each duty share averages the industries' Ontario-wide shares, each weighted by Waterloo's estimated US exports in the industry.

How sure

These are estimates. The method behind them is checked against the exports Statistics Canada publishes for metropolitan areas, including Waterloo's.

  • Kitchener-Cambridge-Waterloo CMA: the exports the method allocates to it are 1.22 times those Statistics Canada publishes: within the bound of half to twice.

The check is of exports, not of the jobs figures above.

Peer group

Waterloo's peer group is urban.
