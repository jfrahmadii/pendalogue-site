About

Pendalogue turns public data into ready-to-use answers for the people who plan and invest in places. We start with Ontario's counties and their exposure to US trade, built for economic development offices, workforce planning boards and the consultants who write their strategies. Profiles covering labour force, economic base and comparisons with similar places come next.

Every figure is read from Statistics Canada and US Census Bureau tables and carries its source and date. Where Statistics Canada publishes no figure for a county, the method is checked against the figures it does publish, for metropolitan areas and rural Ontario. The method and its limits are on the methods page.
