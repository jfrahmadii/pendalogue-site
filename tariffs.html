US tariffs in force on Canadian goods

This page lists the US tariffs the county pages rest on, the industries with the most exports on the tariff lines they list, and Ontario's goods exports to the United States since 2022. Every county page links here; the method is on the methods page.

An industry's share on listed lines is the part of its US goods exports on tariff lines a US instrument in force lists. Listed goods may still enter free under CUSMA (method).
