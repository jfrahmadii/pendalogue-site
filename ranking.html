Ontario's counties by jobs tied to US demand

Counties ranked by the share of their employed residents' jobs that exist because of demand from the United States, in 2022: Ontario's jobs tied to US demand, allocated to counties by the industries their residents work in. The rank is on the share to one decimal; where two counties print the same share, the one with more such jobs comes first. 44 counties are ranked; 5 are not, because the check of the method against published exports failed in their metropolitan area (method). Each county's name links to its page.

Ontario as a whole: about 1,020,400 jobs tied to US demand, 15.7 percent of employed residents; share of US exports in goods that paid US duty, 25.3 percent; on tariff lines a US instrument in force lists, 34.8 percent; US share of exports, 70.5 percent.
